# Soliflex Quarters Manager - Key Features

## Core Functionalities

1. **Residence Management** - Create, view, and update property details including owner information, addresses, and property status

2. **Agreement Management** - Manage employee accommodation agreements with possession dates, renewal tracking, and automatic renewal due date calculation (11 months from possession date)

3. **Employee Management** - Maintain employee profiles with department, designation, and link employees to their allocated agreements

4. **Renewal Alerts** - Automatic tracking of agreement renewals with alerts for past-due agreements and agreements due within 90 days

5. **Financial Tracking** - Monitor total monthly rent, advance amounts locked, advance refunds, and maintenance deductions

6. **Dashboard Analytics** - Real-time overview of total properties, active employees, upcoming renewals, monthly rent, and advance locked amounts

7. **Data Visualization** - Interactive charts showing employee breakdown by department, rent cost by department/designation, and monthly spend trends

8. **Renewal Workflow** - One-click agreement renewal with automatic date suggestions and status updates

9. **Vacate & Refund Management** - Process property vacations, track advance refunds, and calculate net refunds after maintenance deductions

10. **Search & Filter** - Quick search across agreements, residences, and employees with filters for status, renewal dates, and other criteria

11. **Export Reports** - Export data to PDF (dashboard with charts) and Excel (multi-sheet with residences, agreements, employees, and analytics)

12. **Timeline Filtering** - Filter analytics and reports by date ranges (All Time, Last 30/90 Days, Last Quarter, or Custom Range)

13. **Cost Optimization Recommendations** - Automated suggestions for reducing accommodation costs based on property and agreement data

14. **Status Management** - Track Active/Inactive status for residences, agreements, and employees without deleting records

15. **Multi-Device Access** - Responsive design supporting mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers
